How the work actually happens

  1. ResearchInterviews, monthly user groups, and whatever usage data already exists. Mostly to find out which of our assumptions are wrong.
  2. DefineUser stories and acceptance criteria specific enough that engineering never has to guess what "done" means.
  3. AlignGetting engineering, design, and the business to agree on scope before anyone builds the wrong version of it.
  4. IterateEdge cases always surface late. The requirements get rewritten when they do.
